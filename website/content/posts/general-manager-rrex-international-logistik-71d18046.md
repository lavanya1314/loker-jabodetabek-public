<p>PT RREX International Logistik is looking for an experienced and strategic General Manager to lead our business operations, drive company growth, and ensure operational excellence. We are looking for a strong leader with excellent management, analytical, and decision-making skills.</p>
<p><br/></p>
<p><strong>Responsibilities:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Oversee and manage the company's overall business operations to ensure efficiency and productivity.</li>
<li>Develop and implement business strategies, operational plans, and company objectives.</li>
<li>Monitor business performance, operational targets, and key performance indicators (KPIs).</li>
<li>Lead, supervise, and evaluate department heads and teams to achieve company goals.</li>
<li>Manage operational budgets, cost control, and resource allocation.</li>
<li>Identify business opportunities, potential risks, and areas for operational improvement.</li>
<li>Build and maintain strong relationships with clients, business partners, and other stakeholders.</li>
<li>Prepare and present regular business performance reports and strategic recommendations to management.</li>
</ul>
<p><br/></p>
<p><strong>Qualifications:</strong></p>
<p><br/></p>
<ul>
<li>Bachelor's Degree in Business Management, Logistics, Supply Chain Management, or a related field.</li>
<li>Minimum 5–7 years of experience in management or senior leadership roles, preferably in logistics, freight forwarding, or supply chain industries.</li>
<li>Proven experience in managing overall business operations and leading multiple departments.</li>
<li>Strong understanding of business strategy, operational management, budgeting, and financial performance.</li>
<li>Excellent leadership, communication, negotiation, and decision-making skills.</li>
<li>Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.</li>
<li>Ability to develop business strategies, improve operational efficiency, and achieve company targets.</li>
<li>Proactive, result-oriented, and capable of working under pressure.</li>
</ul>
<p><br/></p>
<p>📍 Location: Bizpark – PIK 2, Tangerang</p>
<p>🕘 Working Days: Monday – Saturday (Saturday half day)</p>
