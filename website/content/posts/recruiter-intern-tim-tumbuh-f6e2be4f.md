<p>We’re looking for a proactive and organized <strong>Recruiter Internship</strong> to support recruitment for our clients. You’ll help find suitable candidates, manage recruitment activities, and keep the hiring process moving from sourcing to joining.</p>
<p>Entry-level candidates and fresh graduates are welcome! Experience in mass hiring or high-volume recruitment would be a strong advantage.</p>
<p><strong>What You'll Be Up To</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Align with the TimTumbuh team and client hiring managers on role requirements, priorities, and hiring timelines.</li>
<li>Publish job advertisements and actively source candidates through job portals, social media, communities, and referrals.</li>
<li>Screen applications and conduct initial interviews to understand candidates’ experience, suitability, availability, and salary expectations.</li>
<li>Prepare candidate shortlists and concise interview notes for the team and clients.</li>
<li>Coordinate interviews and keep candidates informed throughout the recruitment process.</li>
<li>Support offer communication and follow up with selected candidates until their joining date.</li>
<li>Maintain candidate records and share regular progress updates against agreed hiring targets.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>What We're Looking For</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Fresh graduates and entry-level candidates are welcome; recruitment experience is a plus.</li>
<li>Strong interest in recruitment and willingness to actively approach potential candidates.</li>
<li>Good communication skills and confidence in conducting initial interviews.</li>
<li>Organized, responsive, and consistent with candidate follow-ups.</li>
<li>Able to work independently, handle multiple vacancies, and meet agreed deadlines.</li>
<li>Comfortable using job portals, social media, and Google Sheets.</li>
<li>Able to handle candidate and client information professionally and confidentially.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Nice-to-Have</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Experience in mass hiring or high-volume recruitment, including internships or freelance projects.</li>
<li>Experience recruiting for different roles, industries, or locations.</li>
<li>Access to candidate networks, job-seeker communities, or other relevant sourcing channels.</li>
</ul>
